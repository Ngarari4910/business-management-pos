# Portfolio Notes

## Why this project is being shown

This repository is the sanitized portfolio version of a business management/POS system used to demonstrate product thinking, AI-assisted development, workflow design, debugging and shipping.

## Suggested Loom walkthrough

Keep the video around 2–3 minutes:

1. Start on the dashboard and explain the business problem.
2. Show inventory / stock intake.
3. Show a cashier sale and receipt flow.
4. Show customer credit or payment reconciliation.
5. Show reporting / management analytics.
6. Briefly show the code structure and tests.
7. Finish with how the same workflow-first approach could be applied to an internal growth tool.
